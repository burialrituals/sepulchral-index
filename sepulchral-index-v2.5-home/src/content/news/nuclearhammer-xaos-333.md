Iron Bonehead Productions will release Nuclearhammer's new EP, *XAOS 333*, on September 25th on CD and 12-inch vinyl.

The three-track recording dates back to 2018 and represents the first Nuclearhammer material to surface since *Xaos Tenewas – Demo MMXXIII* in 2023.

Active for more than two decades, the Toronto trio have continually pushed their bestial black metal foundations toward increasingly unconventional and hypnotic forms. *XAOS 333* continues that trajectory, emphasizing repetition, violent atmosphere and an expansive use of reverberation rather than the more prominent power-electronics elements heard on earlier material such as *Serpentine Hermetic Lucifer*.

According to the label, the EP presents one of Nuclearhammer's most intensely hypnotic manifestations in recent years, with its three compositions moving between abrasive attack and more spacious, trance-inducing passages.
