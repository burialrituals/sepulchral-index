Chilean black metal band ABISMA released *Promo Demo 2026* on March 10, presenting a raw rehearsal recording captured in December 2025.

Conceived as an advance document rather than a polished studio release, the material previews ideas intended for the band's forthcoming full-length. Its stated influences point directly toward the darker and more ritualistic side of early European black metal, particularly Master's Hammer, Varathron, Rotting Christ and early Moonspell.

The current incarnation of ABISMA features Mephistofel on vocals, guitar and keyboards, Aesma Daeva on backing vocals and bass, J. Alvarez on guitars and Old Ripper on drums.
