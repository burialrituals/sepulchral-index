Iron Bonehead Productions has announced **October 30, 2026** as the international release date for *Invoking the Noumenon / Crushing the Feeble Messianic Embrace*, a special split mini-album uniting Greece's **Embrace of Thorns** and Chile's **Invocation**.

The release contains **six exclusive tracks across 22 minutes**, with each band contributing three compositions. Embrace of Thorns return with their first recorded material since 2022's *Entropy Dynamics*, moving between martial, bulldozing bestial Death Metal and a subdued outro that bridges the two sides of the record.

Invocation follow their 2024 debut album *The Archaic Sanctuary (Ritual Body Postures)* with three new tracks rooted in hammering and atmospheric Black/Death Metal, combining a distinctly South American attack with a sepulchral atmosphere.

*Invoking the Noumenon / Crushing the Feeble Messianic Embrace* will be released through **Iron Bonehead Productions** on **CD and 12\" vinyl**.
