Iron Bonehead Productions has announced **October 30, 2026** as the international release date for *Cenotaph Severance*, the fifth full-length from Australia's **Temple Nightside**.

The album marks the band's first full-length since 2020's *Pillars of Damnation*. While retaining the memorable riffing and tightly controlled attack heard on that record, *Cenotaph Severance* pushes deeper into Temple Nightside's characteristic union of ancient death metal, blackened atmosphere and vast cavernous space.

The current lineup also introduces guitarist **Ignus Fatuus**, known for his work with Portal, Impetuous Ritual and Grave Upheaval. The new material reportedly reconnects the band with some of the more bestial impulses heard on the early *Prophecies of Malevolence* demo while maintaining the more developed songwriting of their later work.

*Cenotaph Severance* will be released on **CD and vinyl LP** through Iron Bonehead Productions.
