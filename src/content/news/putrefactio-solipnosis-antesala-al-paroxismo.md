Chilean entities Putrefactio and Solipnosis converge on *Antesala al Paroxismo*, a joint release steeped in esoteric symbolism, ritual violence and uncompromising black metal.

Framed through hermetic and Satanic imagery, the recording presents both bands as parts of the same ceremonial collision rather than as isolated contributions. The conceptual language surrounding the release invokes opposing cosmic forces, solar and lunar symbolism and a broader atmosphere of occult confrontation.

Released by VIRUPI and issued on 12-inch vinyl by IXIOL, *Antesala al Paroxismo* stands firmly within the South American underground tradition of physical, deliberately obscure black metal presentation.
