**NEKUS** and **BLOODSOAKED NECROVOID** join forces on *Funerary Rites of Ancient Doom*, a four-track split spanning 36 minutes of exclusive material and presented on vinyl LP by Caligari Records and Seed of Doom Records.

Germany's NEKUS occupy the first side with a single 19-minute piece, **“Dakhma.”** The track moves between bestial grinding violence and a slower, desolate funerary weight, extending the band's characteristic collision of primitive death metal structures and more abstract tendencies.

BLOODSOAKED NECROVOID take the second side with three new tracks. The Costa Rican-origin band, now relocated to Spain, continue to work within an oppressive cavernous sound where slow-motion grind, death metal and bestial pulses merge into a suffocating atmosphere.

*Funerary Rites of Ancient Doom* brings both bands together in a deliberately sepulchral and expansive split release.
