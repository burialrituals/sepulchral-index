Chile's **Ripper** return with *Towards Rebirth*, their first full-length since 2016's *Experiment of Existence* and the band's third album overall.

The new record retains the razor-sharp guitar work, intense drumming and tightly constructed songwriting associated with Ripper, while pushing the material toward a more concise and vicious form. Dark Descent Records describes *Towards Rebirth* as the band's most violent work to date.

The album was recorded, mixed and mastered by **Pablo Clares at DM6 Studio**, with artwork by **Paolo Girardi**.

*Towards Rebirth* will be released in **2026** through Dark Descent Records on **CD, vinyl and digital formats**.
