Chile should be visible throughout this publication without becoming a border around it. **From Chile** is designed as a permanent route into releases, bands, labels, interviews and historical material that international readers may otherwise encounter only in fragments.

The broader publication remains international. That balance matters: readers may arrive through a Greek death metal record, a German black metal interview or a Japanese doom release and then discover material from Santiago, Valparaíso, Concepción or elsewhere in Chile.

Over time, structured metadata behind each article can turn this coverage into an archive: cities, genres, labels, formats and release years can be explored and eventually summarized through original statistics.
